# Technical report outline

1. Research question and scope.
2. Models, tokenizer and training data, with licenses and revisions.
3. Training compute budgets, controls, optimizer recipe and seeds.
4. Evaluation datasets/tasks, scoring protocol and contamination checks.
5. Measured parameter, artifact, resident, cache and runtime memory.
6. Training, prefill and decode latency/throughput; energy method if available.
7. Results with seed variation and uncertainty.
8. Pareto comparisons at declared resource budgets.
9. Ablations, negative results and implementation limitations.
10. Reproduction commands, software versions, configs and artifact links.

Do not fill this outline with planned or estimated results. Distinguish measured values from analytic estimates and list results that could not be verified.
